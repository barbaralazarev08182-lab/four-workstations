### Universe Enrichment V1

Status: **HUMAN VISUAL FAIL**

The first attempt used low-quality procedural substitutes after an acquisition probe failed. Later diagnosis showed the BlenderKit failure was an invalid `scene_uuid`, not an inability to download the supplied free assets.

Rejected procedural black-hole / nebula / planet placeholders must not become the visual baseline.

### Real-asset black-hole presentation attempt

Status: **HUMAN VISUAL FAIL**

Using the real BlenderKit source and technically expensive Cycles rendering did not make the Unity presentation acceptable. The result read like a glowing gold symbol/logo/portal rather than a convincing astronomical black hole.

Technical provenance, bounce count and “real source asset” status are not quality evidence by themselves.

### Real-asset black-hole + nebula world-space integration

Status: **HUMAN VISUAL FAIL**

The black hole and nebula behaved as finite-distance world-space cards/props and visibly drifted relative to the infinite star background as the player moved.

Rejected route: ordinary finite world-space cards/billboards for ultra-distant celestial phenomena.

### Universe Enrichment V2 — celestial sky lock

Technical sub-result: **PASS**

- black hole / nebula moved into the same camera-only directional sky system as the star field;
- player-translation parallax problem solved / zero-parallax behavior established.

Human visual result: **FAIL**

Reasons:

- black hole far too small and icon-like;
- insufficient visible spacetime/gravitational lensing;
- no strong bending/stretching/arcing of surrounding stars;
- nebula far too small;
- nebula color/luminance too dark and weak;
- distance incorrectly communicated through tiny angular size.

Preserve the sky-lock architecture while replacing the failed visual scale/presentation.

## Current active Outside requirements

- large nearby planet primarily north of Refuge;
- planet may be extremely large but should not constantly dominate primary forward view;
- planet must support high-detail/realistic close apparent scale;
- black hole should be prominent in normal player-visible sky;
- black hole must visibly distort surrounding star/sky sampling;
- giant distant nebula should occupy a large angular area, potentially 25–50% or more of a wide composition and may continue off-frame;
- nebula colors should be clearly visible and rich but not neon;
- astronomical distance is **not** represented by making giant structures tiny;
- celestial layers preserve zero player-translation parallax;
- subtle asynchronous star twinkle and restrained independent cosmic motion are desired;
- visible artificial Outside objects remain non-human/higher-dimensional in origin.
