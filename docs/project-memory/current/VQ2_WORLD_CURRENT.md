# VQ2 World — Current Direction

> Status: **ACTIVE**  
> Updated: 2026-09-08  
> Purpose: compact current world/gameplay truth. Older world-bible, economy, discovery-arc and proposal documents remain supporting history unless explicitly referenced here.

## 1. Core identity

VQ2 is a first-person persistent mystery-room game built around inhabiting a Refuge and repeatedly touching an unknowable Outside.

Core loop:

`Inhabit -> Retrieve -> Inspect -> Use/Place -> Observe -> Connect/Reinterpret`

Design stance:

- LOW VERB / HIGH DISCOVERY;
- complexity comes from world state, relationships, observation and reinterpretation;
- the room is the main interface;
- ordinary life matters: eating, drinking, music, TV, sitting, sleeping, touching/petting the cat, arranging objects, making a home;
- normal domestic life establishes what “normal” means before the Outside gradually destabilizes that understanding.

Avoid turning the game into:

- a survival-bar stack;
- a conventional fishing game;
- fishing QTE / bait tree / lure rarity / giant loot UI;
- restaurant management;
- weapon/fish-killing grind;
- generic cyberpunk UI.

## 2. Refuge / Atlas premise

Humanity's original 3D universe has become or is becoming uninhabitable. A vastly more capable non-human civilization has preserved at least some humans, for motives that should remain uncertain early on.

The player lives inside a small stable 3D Refuge.

Atlas is the stabilizing anchor of that domain:

- Atlas is inside the Refuge;
- a much larger normally invisible spherical stabilized region exists around it;
- the sphere is larger than the architecture;
- walls are domestic/architectural surfaces, not the ultimate physical boundary;
- the sphere maintains local 3D physical continuity inside a higher-dimensional environment.

The player should not begin with a full exposition dump explaining this system.

## 3. Retrieval contract

Retrieval Station + Orb is the controlled interface to the Outside.

Conceptual sequence:

1. the Atlas domain is normally closed/stable;
2. Retrieval opens a controlled interface through the boundary;
3. a powered tether/cable maintains the Orb outside;
4. opening the boundary has a real cost to Refuge stability / mass-energy continuity;
5. Retrieval therefore creates both discovery and resource consequences.

Exact thermodynamic terminology can remain fictionalized; do not casually claim “energy conservation means infinite electricity.”

Retrieved objects should support several kinds of value, potentially at once:

- discovery / curiosity;
- story / research;
- matter-energy-stability recovery;
- repair / fabrication / room building;
- life / relationship / Atlas reaction.

A useful design tension is that dismantling something may be materially valuable while preserving it may unlock more important knowledge or relationships.

## 4. Outside hard rule

The Outside is non-human and higher-dimensional.

**Do not naturally populate it with human leftovers.** In particular, no default:

- old human artifacts;
- human ruins;
- abandoned human stations/infrastructure;
- recovered human records/media drifting outside;
- conventional lost-human-spacecraft archaeology.

Valid Outside-native content includes:

- unfamiliar materials;
- non-human biology;
- alien / higher-dimensional technology;
- constructs;
- samples;
- signals;
- data carriers;
- phenomena that do not map cleanly to human categories.

Artificial structures in the visible Outside, if used, must be non-human / higher-dimensional in origin.

## 5. Outside presentation rule

Use **visible first, activity second**.

The Outside should first be compelling to look at. Authored activity/events should then make it feel inhabited and consequential.

Do not rely on constant explosions, particle spam, or endless motion. Large-scale phenomena can be quiet and still feel alive.

## 6. Two narrative scales

### Player / Refuge line

Questions include:

- why am I here;
- what is this room;
- what are Atlas / Retrieval / power systems;
- why were humans preserved;
- are there other refuges / people;
- how does this place become a home rather than a holding cell.

### Outside / civilization line

The Outside civilization is not a static backdrop. Wider conflicts, preservation ethics, contact and changes in how powerful civilizations treat one another can unfold over time.

When the outside civilization directly controls or communicates through a persistent utility/drone companion, use behavior, movement, voice cadence and concise text/dialogue—not only lore dumps.

## 7. First-hour principle

The first hour should establish control and ownership quickly, reveal a physical Atlas mystery, complete the first Retrieval loop before distant war becomes dominant, and visibly change the room by the end of the hour.

The distant conflict is a playable/world event, not the first thing that replaces domestic orientation.
