# VQ2 World — Current Direction

> Status: **ACTIVE**  
> Updated: 2026-09-08

## Core identity

VQ2 is a first-person persistent mystery-room game about inhabiting a Refuge, reaching into an unknowable Outside, bringing things home, and slowly reinterpreting both the room and the universe around it.

Core loop:

`Inhabit -> Retrieve -> Inspect -> Use/Place -> Observe -> Connect/Reinterpret`

Design stance:

- **LOW VERB / HIGH DISCOVERY**;
- complexity comes from world state, relationships, observation and reinterpretation;
- the room is the main interface;
- ordinary life matters: eating, drinking, music, TV, sitting, sleeping, petting/touching the cat, arranging objects, and making a home;
- normal domestic life establishes what “normal” means before the Outside gradually destabilizes that understanding.

Avoid turning the game into a survival-bar stack, conventional fishing game, fishing-QTE/bait tree, giant loot UI, restaurant-management loop, weapon/fish-killing grind, or generic cyberpunk interface.

## Starting situation

- The player is delivered into the Refuge through the existing left-side entrance by a machine / transfer mechanism.
- The player initially has limited understanding of where the Refuge is, why humans were preserved, or who built the system.
- The Refuge is not meant to arrive as a finished luxury home. It becomes more functional, personal and strange through inhabiting, furnishing, Retrieval, repair and discovery.

## Humanity and the Refuge premise

Humanity's original 3D universe has become or is becoming non-viable. A vastly more capable non-human civilization preserved humans inside stable 3D refuge domains embedded in a higher-dimensional environment.

The exact motives of that civilization should remain uncertain early on. Do not frame preservation as uncomplicated benevolent rescue.

Many human refuges may exist as separate hidden/stabilized domains, but the exact cross-room / Atlas-to-Atlas interaction mechanism remains open and must not be treated as already solved.

## Atlas-centered stabilized domain

Atlas is physically inside the Refuge and acts as the stabilizing anchor/center of a much larger normally invisible spherical 3D domain.

Current world rules:

- the stabilized sphere is substantially larger than the visible house/architecture;
- architecture, atmosphere, inhabitants, matter and familiar local physics remain coherent inside it;
- visible walls are domestic/architectural surfaces, not the ultimate physical boundary;
- the boundary is normally invisible and becomes legible through controlled interaction, distortion or crossing events;
- what humans perceive outside is only a 3D projection/cross-section of a higher-dimensional reality.

The player should experience these rules operationally before receiving a complete cosmology explanation.

## Nested operating fields

The Refuge currently has three conceptually distinct layers:

1. **Atlas Refuge Sphere** — large stabilized 3D domain.
2. **Wireless Power Field** — smaller local sphere generated by the wall-mounted Power Hub; ordinary Refuge devices and the guide drone can operate inside it without conventional plug-to-wall wiring.
3. **Powered Retrieval Tether** — narrow controlled connection allowing the Retrieval Orb to operate beyond the normal local fields and cross the Atlas boundary.

Do not collapse these three systems into one generic force field.

## Closed-domain conservation intent

When the Atlas sphere is fully closed, the Refuge behaves fictionally like a sealed mass-energy system. The user intent is that ordinary local power can feel effectively persistent/stable while the Refuge remains closed.

Do not overclaim real-world thermodynamics or explain this as literal perpetual motion. Exact fictional terminology remains open.

The important gameplay rule is that deliberately opening the Refuge to the Outside has a real cost: dimensional bleed, stability expenditure, mass/energy loss, entropy cost, or another later-defined mechanism.

## Retrieval contract

Retrieval Station + Orb is the controlled interface through the Atlas boundary.

Conceptual sequence:

1. the Atlas domain is normally closed/stable;
2. Retrieval creates a temporary controlled crossing/interface;
3. the powered tether maintains the Orb outside;
4. opening the boundary costs Refuge stability/resources;
5. an Outside-native target is coupled/retrieved;
6. the catch is stabilized into the Refuge as a real physical object/sample;
7. the player decides whether to use, process, preserve, place, study or otherwise respond to it.

Retrieved objects should support multiple forms of value, potentially at once:

- discovery / curiosity;
- story / research / knowledge;
- matter-energy-stability recovery;
- repair / fabrication / room building;
- life / relationship / Atlas reaction;
- persistent visual identity in the room.

A strong recurring tension is that dismantling something may be materially useful while preserving it may unlock more important knowledge, behavior or relationships.

## Outside hard rule

The Outside is non-human and higher-dimensional.

**Do not naturally populate it with human leftovers.** No default human ruins, abandoned stations, human spacecraft archaeology, records/media, furniture, tools or ordinary human artifacts drifting outside.

Valid Outside-native content includes unfamiliar matter, organisms, condensates, non-human constructs/technology, samples, signals/data carriers, and phenomena that do not map cleanly to human categories.

Visible artificial structures in the Outside must be non-human / higher-dimensional in origin.

## Outside presentation rule

Use **visible first, activity second**.

The Outside should already be compelling to look at before authored events occur. Large-scale phenomena can be quiet; do not rely on constant explosions, particle spam or endless motion.

## Two narrative scales

### Player / Refuge line

Questions include:

- why am I here;
- what is this room;
- what are Atlas, Power and Retrieval;
- why were humans preserved;
- are there other refuges / people;
- how does this place become a home rather than a holding cell.

### Outside / civilization line

The Outside civilization is an active narrative force. Wider conflict, preservation ethics, contact and changing relationships between powerful civilizations may unfold over time.

When the civilization directly controls or communicates through the persistent utility/drone companion, show the change through behavior, movement, gaze/orientation, timing, voice cadence and concise text/dialogue rather than lore dumps alone.

## Reveal principle

Prefer:

`physical evidence -> system behavior -> player inference -> partial explanation -> later reinterpretation`

Avoid front-loading the full Atlas cosmology, conservation rule, alien motives or surviving-human count in opening exposition.
