## Top-view provisional assessment

The zoning direction is materially better than the previous single-hall reading, especially around Experiment and Sleep, but the top view also suggests a risk: some open frames may read too literally as freestanding wireframe boxes / exhibition structures rather than refined residential spatial boundaries.

Therefore:
- Do not treat the current Living or Sleep frame geometry as accepted final language.
- First-person review should determine whether frames need to be reduced, fragmented, replaced by cabinetry/slats/material/lighting, or removed.
- Experiment zoning currently appears to be the strongest architectural boundary concept and should be used as the quality reference, not copied mechanically to every zone.
