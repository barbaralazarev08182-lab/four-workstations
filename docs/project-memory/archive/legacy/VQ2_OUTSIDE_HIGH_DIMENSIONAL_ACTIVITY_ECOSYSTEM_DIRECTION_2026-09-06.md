# VQ2 Outside High-Dimensional Activity Ecosystem Direction — 2026-09-06

## Core lock

The high-dimensional Outside is not an empty backdrop around the human Refuge. It is a living, inhabited, industrial, ecological, commercial and infrastructural space with many independent civilizations and lifeforms. Human Refuge worlds are only one tiny hidden phenomenon inside that larger universe.

The player sees only 3D projections / slices of high-dimensional objects. High-dimensional life, vehicles, megastructures and events therefore must not behave visually like ordinary 3D objects placed in a skybox.

Human-made objects do not naturally exist Outside. Human furniture, cameras, speakers, records, cards, tools and other lifestyle objects must be manufactured inside the Refuge. Outside can provide high-dimensional native materials, biological shed matter, industrial byproducts, exotic matter, samples, fragments and other non-human resources that can later be stabilized, processed and used to manufacture human-designed objects.

## Life diversity

Outside life can include many forms, not only translucent / jellyfish-like organisms:

- carbon-like soft-bodied organisms
- silicon-based life
- metallic / mineral-bodied life
- crystalline life
- plasma / field-like life
- long ribbon / filament / worm-like organisms
- radial / polyhedral / topology-changing organisms
- humanoid-like silhouettes when their high-dimensional anatomy projects that way
- massive solitary megafauna
- microscopic or very small swarms
- enormous migration groups numbering in the thousands or millions
- colony organisms whose apparent individuals merge/split in 3D projection
- technological life or organisms integrated with vehicles / artificial structures

Visual variety must still preserve a coherent projection grammar: topology changes, apparent merge/split, inconsistent scale cues, impossible occlusion, discontinuous visible sections, non-Euclidean apparent motion and projection-driven silhouette changes.

## Civilizational activity

The Outside should routinely show signs of active civilization, not only rare wars. Possible activity classes:

- interstellar / inter-system trade routes
- cargo convoys
- passenger / migration fleets
- survey craft
- maintenance craft
- construction fleets
- mining operations
- energy harvesting
- planetary drilling / extraction
- asteroid processing
- star-lifting or stellar material collection
- orbital or system-scale industrial platforms
- acceleration-ring / transit-ring infrastructure
- large traffic hubs and transfer corridors
- scientific observation arrays
- refuge-maintenance craft belonging to the civilization that preserved humanity
- rare conflict / war / disaster events

## Megastructures and large engineering

Potential spectacles include:

- stellar-energy collector swarms / shells around a star
- multiple sequential acceleration rings used to increase transit velocity
- planet-boring extraction structures that visibly penetrate and process a planet
- asteroid-belt harvesters that redirect and disassemble large numbers of bodies
- star-lifting structures extracting stellar plasma/material
- huge transit gates / transfer corridors
- distributed light-harvesting lattices spanning a planetary system
- construction frames around moons or planets
- moving industrial habitats / mobile megastructures

These should not be direct copies of existing fiction; use original silhouettes, operating logic and high-dimensional projection behavior.

## Event hierarchy

Outside content should be distributed across four levels:

1. Ambient traffic / background life
   - frequent, small, usually non-interactive
   - distant convoys, small migration groups, maintenance craft, mining lights, traffic lanes
   - purpose: make the Outside continuously alive

2. Readable recurring activities
   - medium frequency and duration
   - trade convoys, migration waves, extraction cycles, construction operations
   - player can learn to recognize them over time

3. Opportunity events
   - some events naturally produce catchable / collectable high-dimensional shed matter, industrial byproducts, fragments or samples
   - no quest marker required; visual event should itself explain why fishing / Retrieval becomes relevant

4. Chapter-scale spectacles / climaxes
   - rare
   - giant organism passage, stellar collision, supernova, large fleet arrival, war, disaster, refuge-maintenance visit, discovery of the Refuge entrance
   - major visual and narrative events, not routine farming content

## Design rule: spectacle first, gameplay second

Every major Outside event should first read as a believable thing happening independently in the universe. Gameplay opportunities may emerge from it, but the event should not look staged only for the player.

Example:
A huge migration crosses the visible sky -> some organisms shed luminous / structural material -> Fishing Station shows subtle coupling response -> player may choose to collect a sample -> sample can be stabilized and processed into useful material.

## Design rule: not everything is interactive

Many events should be purely observable. The player must be allowed to simply watch. If every ship, creature or stellar event produces loot or a mission, the universe will feel like a game-content dispenser rather than an independent world.

## Design rule: traffic and civilization need recurrence

Trade lanes, cargo traffic, construction and mining should recur enough that the player can develop familiarity:

- recognize a common freight pattern
- distinguish survey craft from cargo traffic
- notice when a usual route is unexpectedly empty
- understand that a rare military movement is abnormal because ordinary civilian activity has been established first

This baseline makes later crises and wars more impactful.

## Fishing / collection relationship

Fishing should target physically or projection-logically plausible small material opportunities near the Refuge boundary:

- biological shed fibers
- molted shells / plates
- crystalline condensation
- metallic scales / fragments from metallic organisms
- industrial dust / slag / lattice fragments from nearby traffic or extraction
- non-living exotic matter drifting through the coupling region

Do not allow fishing to produce intact human artifacts.

## Production relationship

Outside material -> stabilize / process inside Refuge -> use to manufacture human objects.

Examples:
- elastic high-dimensional fiber -> speaker diaphragm / advanced line / flexible structure
- optical crystal -> camera lens / sensor
- conductive lattice -> electronics / drone components / audio hardware
- stable luminous material -> lighting / markers
- hard metallic-biological plate -> structural shell / tool component

The final manufactured product should look intentionally human-designed, contrasting with the strange native appearance of Outside matter.

## Long-term tone

Outside must support a full spectrum:

- mundane everyday traffic
- ecological beauty
- industrial scale
- peaceful civilization
- strange life
- awe
- occasional danger
- rare catastrophe

War is a minority event. The universe should feel inhabited long before it feels threatened.

## Authoritative direction

Future Outside-event design should prioritize:

1. World logic and visual readability
2. Original high-dimensional projection behavior
3. Independent life/civilization activity
4. Strong spectacle and scale
5. Selective, natural gameplay opportunities
6. Integration with Fishing / Retrieval / Processing / Creation without turning every event into loot

Do not reduce Outside to a static skybox, random VFX show, or constant resource farm.
