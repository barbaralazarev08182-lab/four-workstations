- Retrieval Station on east/open side around X=7.25, Z=1.5.

The V3 task is not a layout reset. It is an architectural-massing redesign around these anchors.

## 2. V3 spatial principle

The 20x20 room must stop reading as one giant hall with scattered furniture.

Use three scale bands:

- ~6m: refuge shell / main walls.
- ~2.2-2.5m: human-scale architectural masses such as cabinetry, boiserie, atelier storage, privacy volumes, and room-defining elements.
- ~0.4-1.0m: furniture-scale objects.

Do not use full rectangular overhead frames as the primary zoning language.

## 3. Required massing studies

### Entry
Create a shallow arrival threshold rather than a bare door in the wall. Use a bench / low storage mass plus one taller storage/coat volume. Keep circulation open and do not form a corridor.

### Living
Keep TV and living position. Replace the weak small-object reading with a larger residential composition:

- TV wall as a 4.2-4.8m-wide integrated boiserie / plaster / cabinet massing study.
- Sofa system may be tested at roughly 4.2-4.8m overall scale.
- Living rug may be tested around 5.2x4.6m.
- Keep sofa-back low storage as a room edge.
- No full overhead frame.

### Experiment / Material Atelier
Make this the strongest room-without-walls condition:

- north and west sides read as integrated 2.3-2.45m-high work/storage architecture;
- south edge uses low cabinetry around 0.8-0.9m with only partial upper slats/ribbed-glass massing;
- east edge must use a different language, such as open shelving or a translucent/sliding panel mass;
- preserve a deliberate 1.5-1.8m entrance;
- do not duplicate the same screen treatment on every edge.

### Window Room
Unify Pantry -> medium cabinet -> low cabinet/bench -> Window -> Bar into one composition. Use tall-to-medium-to-low mass hierarchy. Preserve white wall negative space and do not fill the entire north wall.

### Sleep / Private Rest
Create a protected alcove, not a bed plus free-standing screen:

- strengthen headboard/storage boundary;
- add one 2.2-2.4m-height wardrobe/privacy volume;
- one additional softer or lighter boundary may be represented with slats/curtain/translucent massing;
- keep the southeast/outside-facing corner open;
- no full room enclosure and no full overhead frame.

## 4. Areas that remain intentionally open

- Atlas: no border, frame, cabinet, fence, rug ring, or other explicit zoning device.
- Retrieval: no vertical enclosure; preserve Orb/tether/deploy/landing/moving-object operation space. A floor-material transition may be studied later, but not if it affects gameplay.
- South/open edge: preserve meaningful negative space.

## 5. Material-language placeholder guidance

Massing may use restrained neutral placeholders to suggest future hierarchy only:

- warm white mineral/plaster mass,
- light-oak-like mass,
- warm stone-like mass,
- neutral translucent/ribbed-glass-like mass,
- off-white/greige soft-furniture mass.

Avoid bright debug colors, black industrial framing, cyberpunk styling, and final material authoring.
