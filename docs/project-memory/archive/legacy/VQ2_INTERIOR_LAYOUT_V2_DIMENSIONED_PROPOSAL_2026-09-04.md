# VQ2 Interior Layout V2 — Dimensioned Proposal — 2026-09-04

> Status: PROPOSAL / NOT YET USER-LOCKED
>
> Scope: 20x20m Open20m residential + experiment + Retrieval layout, using the current accepted room shell.
>
> Coordinate frame: floor X,Z in [-10,10], West wall X=-10, North wall Z=+10, East and South open. Wall height remains approximately 6m. Existing Atlas and Retrieval objects should not be moved unless a later Human Spatial review explicitly requires it.

## 1. User direction carried forward

- Entrance remains on the West wall, biased toward the South/lower portion.
- TV moves to the West wall, on the wall segment to the right/north of the entrance.
- Sofa faces the West-wall TV and forms a floating residential island rather than hugging a wall.
- The two-wall corner is the experiment/workshop zone, not a dining zone.
- North-wall east/right side receives a large window with a domestic bar/counter in front.
- Retrieval remains on the East/open side.
- The overall interior should read primarily as a premium Italian/Scandinavian home, with experiment and Retrieval functions integrated without turning the room into an office or laboratory.

## 2. Proposed dimensioned layout

### Entrance

Candidate center: approximately `(-9.965, 1.4, -6.6)`.

Door face size: `1.4m x 2.8m`.

This leaves about 2.7m from the South/open floor edge to the near door jamb and creates a small arrival buffer before the living room opens.

### West-wall TV / media zone

TV center along the West wall: approximately `Z=-1.8m`.

Suggested first-pass screen width: `2.2-2.5m` (large residential display, not cinema wall).

Suggested media console footprint: approximately `3.2-3.6m` long x `0.40-0.48m` deep.

Keep the media composition visually low, approximately under `2.2-2.4m` total visual height, so the 6m wall retains generous white negative space above.

### Living / sofa island

Sofa center: approximately `(-5.5, 0, -1.8)`.

Suggested sofa envelope: approximately `3.2-3.6m` long along Z x `0.95-1.10m` deep along X.

Preferred first pass: straight or gently modular sofa rather than a large L-shape, to preserve circulation from entry toward Atlas and the North side.

Suggested rug envelope: approximately `4.4 x 3.6m`.

Suggested coffee table envelope: approximately `1.2-1.5m x 0.65-0.9m`.

Leave roughly `0.6-0.8m` practical access around the coffee-table group and at least `1.2-1.5m` behind the sofa where it acts as a circulation edge.

### Experiment / workshop corner

Anchor the experiment zone in the West/North wall corner.

Suggested usable zone:

- X: approximately `-9.3 to -3.8`
- Z: approximately `4.6 to 9.4`
- effective footprint: about `5.5 x 4.8m`

Use the two real walls as the primary enclosure. Do not build a full third wall.

Suggested L-shaped work composition:

- main Matter/analysis bench on the North wall, around `2.6-3.2m` long and `0.8-0.9m` deep;
- secondary support/storage bench on the West wall, around `2.4-3.0m` long and `0.65-0.8m` deep;
- hidden lower storage rather than exposed office shelving.

Suggested semi-open divider toward the room:

- one primary screen approximately `2.4-3.2m` long;
- height approximately `1.5-1.8m`;
- optionally combine a `0.75-0.9m` low cabinet with slats, textured/smoked glass, or light vertical fins above;
- keep at least one broad diagonal/open entry so the experiment corner never reads as a boxed laboratory.

The experiment corner must visually hide routine clutter while preserving light, sightlines, and the 6m-high continuous room volume.

### North-wall window + bar

Window center: approximately `X=5.4 to 5.6m`.

Suggested window width: `5.2-5.8m`.

Suggested sill height: `0.85-0.95m`.

Suggested window height: `2.1-2.4m`.

Suggested domestic bar/counter:

- length: `3.8-4.4m`;
- depth: `0.55-0.70m`;
- top height: roughly `1.05-1.12m` if using true bar-height stools;
- 3 stools are preferred for the first pass;
- keep approximately `0.65-0.75m` center spacing per stool and roughly `0.9-1.2m` clear circulation behind occupied stools.

This area should support eating, drinking, observing Outside, plants, conversation, and later cat activity rather than becoming a dedicated commercial bar.

### Retrieval zone

Keep the existing Retrieval location unless Human review reveals a conflict.

Current reference center: approximately `(7.25, 1.5)` in X/Z.

Retain approximately `4.5 x 6m` of functional operating envelope around it for:

- player stance;
- Station arm / forward socket direction;
- tether deployment toward the open East side;
- future Echo display;
- retrieved-object landing and handling.

Do not place residential furniture in the direct tether/deployment lane.

### Atlas

Keep Atlas at or very near `(0,0)` for this V2.

Do not treat the previous 7x7m Atlas zone as a literal empty square. Instead preserve a soft approximately `3-3.5m` radius of strong negative space around Atlas while allowing circulation paths to pass naturally around it.

Most important sightline rule: Atlas must not sit on the direct TV-to-sofa viewing axis.

With the proposed West-wall TV and sofa center near X=-5.5, the current Atlas center at X=0 naturally remains behind the sofa/living island rather than blocking the TV view.

### South/open-edge quiet observe area

Do not create a large formal zone.

Use one small residential moment only, for example:

- one chaise or two lounge chairs;
- one small side table;
- optional floor lamp / plant;
- placed roughly around `X=-1 to +2`, `Z=-7 to -8.5`, subject to first-person review.

This preserves a direct domestic relationship to the open Outside without competing with the main sofa zone.

## 3. Circulation rules

Target the following clear paths:

- Entry -> Living: at least `1.2-1.5m` clear.
- Entry -> Atlas / central room: at least `1.5m` clear.
- Atlas -> Retrieval: approximately `1.5m` or more.
- Retrieval -> Experiment: maintain an unobstructed logistics path; target `1.5m` clear minimum and avoid routing through the sofa/rug island.
- Bar stool rear clearance: target approximately `0.9-1.2m`.
- Experiment entry: at least `1.2m` clear.

The room should feel intentionally open, not merely empty.

## 4. Residential style intent

Primary visual ratio:

`~80% premium domestic residence / ~20% extraordinary functional technology`.

Ordinary furniture should use a restrained palette:

- off-white / light greige upholstery;
- current pale warm oak floor;
- white walls;
- light natural textiles;
- limited warm stone / travertine-like surfaces;
- warm titanium / champagne metal as small accents;
- very limited graphite/dark elements.

Do not make every ordinary object futuristic. Atlas and Retrieval should remain the primary extraordinary hero objects; the experiment zone should feel like a refined atelier/material studio, not an office cubicle or industrial lab.

## 5. Implementation boundary

This document is a dimensioned proposal only.

Do not yet:

- cut the final door opening;
- create final window architecture;
- build final furniture assets;
- move Atlas or Retrieval without review;
- build the final Matter Bench;
- modify lighting;
- promote to production.

Recommended next implementation is a candidate-only Interior Layout V2 proxy pass using simple blocks for door, TV/media console, sofa/rug/coffee table, experiment benches/screen, window/bar/stools, and one small observe seating group.

## 6. Human review questions

The V2 pass should be judged from first person for:

- whether entry reveals the room gradually;
- whether TV/sofa feel residential and intimate despite 20x20m scale;
- whether the experiment corner feels integrated rather than laboratory-like;
- whether Retrieval remains operationally distinct but visually compatible;
- whether bar/window has enough breathing room;
- whether Retrieval-to-experiment logistics are short and unobstructed;
- whether Atlas remains a central presence without blocking domestic sightlines.
