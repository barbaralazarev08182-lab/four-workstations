# VQ2 Refuge Sphere, Power & Retrieval Narrative Contract — 2026-09-03

Status: USER NARRATIVE DIRECTION — CORE RULES LOCKED / ATLAS CROSS-ROOM MECHANISM STILL OPEN

This document records the user's latest explicit narrative rules and connects them to the existing VQ2 world premise. It does not lock the exact Atlas-to-another-room interaction mechanism.

## 1. Starting situation

- The player is delivered into the room through the existing left-side door by a machine / transfer mechanism.
- The player begins with limited understanding of where the room is, why humanity was preserved, or who created this refuge.
- The room is not meant to arrive as a fully finished luxury home. The player gradually explores, restores, builds, furnishes, and personalizes it with chairs, tables, domestic devices, infrastructure, and recovered materials.

## 2. Humanity and the refuge premise

- Humanity's original three-dimensional universe is being destroyed / has become non-viable.
- A non-human civilization preserved at least some humans by placing them inside a protected three-dimensional refuge created within a higher-dimensional environment.
- This is not yet framed as simple benevolent rescue; the alien civilization's motives remain unknown.

## 3. Atlas-centered refuge sphere — LOCKED DIRECTION

- Atlas is physically located inside the room and acts as the center of a much larger invisible spherical domain.
- The sphere radius must be substantially larger than the room / house radius, currently envisioned as at least more than twice the house radius.
- The sphere is normally transparent / invisible to the player.
- Its purpose is not merely a conventional force shield. It defines and preserves a stable three-dimensional volume inside the higher-dimensional environment.
- The house, atmosphere, inhabitants, ordinary matter, and local physical rules exist safely inside this Atlas-centered stabilized 3D volume.
- This rule gives a narrative reason why the architectural room can become open on some sides without requiring conventional enclosing walls everywhere: the true habitable boundary is the larger Atlas sphere, not necessarily the visible house walls.

## 4. Wall-mounted wireless power hub — LOCKED DIRECTION

- A large wall-mounted power unit / battery is planned as the room's local wireless power distributor.
- It creates a smaller spherical power-coverage region centered on itself.
- Devices inside this power field can operate without ordinary plug-to-wall wiring.
- The small guide drone is intended to operate within this wireless field and can use it as its normal power environment.
- The wall power hub is distinct from the much larger Atlas refuge sphere.

## 5. Closed refuge conservation rule — LOCKED INTENT

- When the Atlas refuge sphere remains fully closed, the refuge behaves as a sealed mass-energy system.
- Matter and energy remain within the protected volume rather than freely escaping into the higher-dimensional exterior.
- The user's intended gameplay fiction is that ordinary room power can therefore feel effectively inexhaustible while the refuge remains sealed and stable.
- Exact thermodynamic language is not yet locked; future lore should preserve the intended closed-system stability without requiring a literal real-world perpetual-motion explanation.

## 6. Retrieval requires breaching the refuge — LOCKED DIRECTION

- The Retrieval Station and powered Orb are the controlled mechanism by which the room deliberately reaches beyond the protected Atlas sphere.
- To deploy the Orb outside, the system must create a temporary controlled opening / passage through the otherwise sealed refuge boundary.
- The powered tether provides the Orb with energy while it operates outside the room's normal protected / wireless-power environment.
- Therefore Retrieval is not merely recreational fishing. Every expedition is a controlled violation of the refuge's isolation.

## 7. Opening cost / dimensional loss — LOCKED INTENT

- Opening the Atlas boundary causes a loss from the refuge system.
- The user currently frames this as mass / energy loss associated with opening a hole in the closed protected domain.
- Exact fictional physics remain open: it may later be described as matter leakage, dimensional bleed, stability expenditure, metric loss, entropy cost, or another original mechanism.
- The important gameplay rule is locked: opening the refuge to retrieve something must have a real systemic cost.

## 8. Retrieved objects must support survival and growth — LOCKED DIRECTION

Recovered content cannot exist only as collectibles or plot props.

Retrieval has at least three simultaneous purposes:

1. discovery / entertainment / curiosity;
2. narrative progression and strange-object investigation;
3. replenishing the refuge's material / energy / stability needs so the room can continue operating and developing.

This allows recovered matter to support:

- maintaining the refuge after opening losses;
- construction and furnishing;
- infrastructure growth;
- fabrication / repair;
- future domestic life systems;
- Atlas development;
- research and story discoveries.

Not every recovered object needs to be consumed as raw resource. Some may instead be preserved because their knowledge, behavior, relationship, life, or narrative value is more important than their mass value.

## 9. Strong resulting gameplay tension

The conservation rule creates a meaningful choice:

> A recovered object may be valuable as matter / energy support, but it may also be more valuable if preserved, studied, placed in the room, connected to Atlas, or allowed to remain alive.

This supports VQ2's Low Verb / High Discovery direction because the depth can come from what the player chooses to do with the same recovered object rather than from adding more control verbs.

## 10. Nested-field structure

The current world can be understood as three nested operating domains:

- **Atlas Refuge Sphere** — large invisible domain that preserves stable 3D habitation inside a higher-dimensional exterior;
- **Wireless Power Sphere** — smaller local field produced by the wall-mounted power hub for normal room devices and the guide drone;
- **Powered Retrieval Tether** — narrow controlled energy connection that allows the Orb to operate beyond the normal local fields and cross the Atlas boundary.

This nested structure should remain visually and mechanically legible over time.

## 11. Atlas cross-room interaction — NOT LOCKED

The exact method by which Atlas eventually interacts with another person's / another survivor's room remains unresolved.

Do not yet lock:

- whether Atlas directly shows another room;
- whether the rooms exchange matter;
- whether the connection is visual, spatial, informational, temporal, or topological;
- whether the other room belongs to another human, another preserved species, or some other entity;
- the exact player-to-player interaction verbs.

However, a promising compatibility direction exists: if each refuge has an Atlas-like stabilizing anchor, Atlas-to-Atlas interaction could emerge from the fact that all refuge spheres are nodes embedded in the same higher-dimensional substrate. This is a design proposal only, not yet a user-locked rule.

## 12. Compatibility with earlier Atlas work

Earlier Atlas design treated Atlas as a mysterious long-term structure whose physical growth gradually reveals a persistent connection to another real room. That remains usable as a design possibility, but the new refuge-sphere function gives Atlas a stronger primary purpose: it may first be the mechanism that keeps the player's 3D world physically possible, with cross-room behavior emerging later as a deeper capability rather than being its obvious initial purpose.

## 13. Narrative principle

The player should initially experience the system operationally before understanding its cosmology:

arrival -> restore / inhabit room -> discover wireless power -> build ordinary life -> discover Atlas behaviors -> learn that the visible house is not the true boundary -> use Retrieval -> notice the cost of opening the refuge -> recover matter and anomalies -> infer why the system exists -> only later approach the truth of the alien civilization and other refuge nodes.

Do not explain all of these rules through opening exposition. Prefer physical evidence, system behavior, environmental reactions, and later reinterpretation.

## 14. Inspiration boundary

The high-dimensional refuge and conservation ideas are inspiration points only. VQ2 should develop original terminology, fictional physics, alien motives, mechanics, and reveal structure rather than copying distinctive plot mechanisms or terminology from existing fiction.
