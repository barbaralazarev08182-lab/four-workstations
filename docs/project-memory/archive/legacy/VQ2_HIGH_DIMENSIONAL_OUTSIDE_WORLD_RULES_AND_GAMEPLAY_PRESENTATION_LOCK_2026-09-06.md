# VQ2 High-Dimensional Outside — World Rules and Gameplay Presentation Lock

Date: 2026-09-06

## Core world rules

- The player's refuge is a small stable 3D world/sphere created inside a higher-dimensional universe by the non-human civilization that preserved humans.
- Many humans were preserved in separate 3D refuge worlds of this kind.
- These 3D refuge worlds are normally invisible/unreachable from the higher-dimensional outside unless their entrance/interface is found.
- Therefore distant wars or events in the Outside do not automatically damage the refuge.
- What the human player sees outside is only a 3D projection/cross-section understandable to human perception, not the true higher-dimensional geometry.
- Human-made objects do not naturally exist in the Outside. Human devices/furniture/records must be made or reconstructed inside the refuge.
- Fishing/Retrieval must therefore never pull ordinary human relics from the Outside. Valid targets are native higher-dimensional matter, organisms, condensates, non-human constructs/technology, samples, signals/data carriers, or other Outside-native phenomena.

## Outside spectacle/content direction

The Outside should provide recurring but authored spectacular events, including examples such as:
- gigantic non-hostile organisms whose projected scale can exceed a galaxy;
- mass migration of native high-dimensional life;
- supernova-like events and stellar-scale explosions;
- meteor/comet/asteroid-belt disruption events;
- collisions between stars or other large-scale celestial structures;
- distant wars between higher-dimensional civilizations/species;
- ships from the civilization that created the human refuges arriving to inspect/stabilize a refuge;
- encounters with outside organisms/entities that can perceive the hidden refuge without attacking it;
- rare hazards or disasters where the player must avoid exposure, stabilize the refuge, or survive until outside help arrives.

These events must have visual climax, clear staging, and gameplay consequences when appropriate; they cannot be generic background skybox decoration.

## Fishing correction

Fishing must be visually and mechanically coherent with the high-dimensional Outside:
- The player should first see a valid target/phenomenon in the projected Outside and naturally understand why it can be fished/coupled.
- Fishing does not retrieve ordinary rocks only for generic energy and must never retrieve human artifacts.
- A caught result should be an Outside-native sample/material/organism fragment/condensate/structure that can be stabilized into 3D inside the refuge.
- Processing should have a concrete purpose: the result can become useful material/components for human-made devices or home objects, or unlock/enable specific later interactions.
- The presentation must make the transition from high-dimensional target -> boundary coupling -> stabilized 3D catch -> processing feel physically coherent.

## Drone lock

The drone is a major persistent interaction and narrative object.
- Even when not controlled/possessed by the outside civilization, it remains an autonomous interactive voice-controlled companion/utility drone.
- Future implementation may use an embedded small speech-recognition / voiceprint / intent model for local voice control.
- The drone should support practical commands, observation, following/positioning, lighting/scanning/recording assistance, and narrative interaction.
- When the outside civilization takes control, the change should be communicated through motion language, timing, gaze/orientation, voice cadence, text presentation, and behavior — not only exposition text.

## Immersion requirements

Immersion is multi-layered and requires coordinated presentation across:
- image clarity and stylized visual language;
- high-dimensional projection effects and cosmic animation;
- room object animation and physical responses;
- drone movement/animation;
- voice interaction;
- subtitle/text layout, typography, timing, and content;
- dialogue writing;
- environmental sound/music;
- camera framing and player freedom during story beats;
- gameplay interactions that are physically coherent with what is shown.

Major story beats should avoid abstract lore dumps. Whenever possible, the player should witness the event in the Outside, receive only necessary interpretation from the drone/system, and remain physically present/free in the room.

## Design consequence

Future gameplay/content design must be checked against these rules. In particular:
- no human artifacts naturally appearing in Outside fishing/retrieval;
- high-dimensional phenomena should not be treated as ordinary 3D space objects with only weird textures;
- Outside events should use projection-specific cues such as topology changes, impossible self-occlusion, parts merging/splitting, non-Euclidean apparent motion, and inconsistent projected scale where appropriate;
- gameplay, visuals, story presentation, sound, text, and drone behavior must be designed together for climax scenes.
