# VQ2 Interior Zoning & Partition Architecture Research — 2026-09-04

> Status: RESEARCHED DESIGN PROPOSAL / NOT YET USER-LOCKED
>
> Scope: Open20m candidate after V2.1 Domestic Completion Graybox. This note addresses the user's explicit requirement that the 20x20 room must not read as one undifferentiated giant hall. Zones should feel spatially distinct through architectural and interior-design means without turning the refuge into many closed rooms.

## 1. Core finding

High-end Italian / Scandinavian open-plan interiors do not rely on one method of separation. They preserve visual continuity while creating 'room-like' zones using a hierarchy of architectural objects, cabinetry, partial partitions, ceiling or overhead markers, rugs/material fields, lighting, furniture orientation, and negative space.

Relevant references reviewed:

- Norm Architects — Vigi House: open plan is broken up by distinctive architectural elements / monoliths that create flow rather than enclosed rooms.
- Norm Architects — Kent Avenue Penthouse: open plan is layered with elements that subtly define zones while maintaining fluidity.
- Poliform — Harmony in Details: architectural doorways divide and connect spaces; shelving systems have an architectural presence.
- Poliform — Via Montello: a marble partition separates kitchen and living while keeping the larger social space open.
- Molteni&C — Living Box / 505 UP: storage and shelving systems behave like small pieces of architecture and become spatial anchors.
- ArchDaily — Minas Apartment: partly slatted / partly solid panel defines zones while preserving visual permeability.
- ArchDaily — Alcantara Apartment: floor-to-ceiling partition varies in height to keep light and visual connection; dining is distinguished through ceiling lighting.
- ArchDaily — How to Divide Spaces Without Traditional Solid Partitions: lowered/suspended ceilings, level shifts, material change and hanging elements can mark zones without conventional walls.

## 2. Design principle for VQ2

The target is not 'one 20x20m hall with furniture'.

The target is:

> A sequence of rooms without fully closing the room.

Each zone should have at least two reinforcing boundary cues selected from:

1. partial architectural wall / portal / frame;
2. built-in cabinetry / storage spine / shelving monolith;
3. slatted / textured-glass / translucent divider;
4. low cabinet / console that establishes an edge;
5. rug or floor field;
6. overhead canopy / suspended frame / lowered visual ceiling;
7. dedicated light composition;
8. furniture orientation and back-of-sofa boundary;
9. controlled negative-space gap between zones;
10. plant or sculptural object used as a secondary separator.

Do not give every zone the same screen type. Repetition would make the layout look like a showroom of dividers rather than architecture.

## 3. Boundary hierarchy

Use three levels of separation.

### Level A — Strong room-like separation

Use for Experiment and Private Rest.

Goal: clearly feel like entering another room, while preserving overhead openness and some visual continuity.

Candidate tools:
- L-shaped built-in cabinetry;
- 1.8–2.2m high slatted / textured-glass / mixed solid-open divider;
- a 2.6–3.0m visual ceiling/canopy/frame below the ~6m main wall height;
- a distinct internal lighting layer;
- intentional portal/opening rather than an undefined edge.

### Level B — Medium spatial definition

Use for Living and Window/Bar/Pantry.

Goal: feel clearly bounded but social and open.

Candidate tools:
- large rug field;
- sofa and console acting as a spatial edge;
- media wall or full-length low cabinet;
- suspended light or ceiling frame aligned to the furniture island;
- cabinetry / pantry block as a vertical anchor;
- a deliberate 1–1.5m negative-space gap before the next zone.

### Level C — Soft / gameplay-aware definition

Use for Dining, Observe and Retrieval.

Goal: establish identity without obstructing long views or gameplay.

Candidate tools:
- pendant light over table;
- small rug / low furniture composition;
- low console or freestanding shelving edge;
- sculptural plant / side table;
- lighting pool;
- for Retrieval, a low architectural edge or overhead portal/frame only if it does not interfere with tether/deployment/landing.

## 4. Zone-specific recommendation for current V2.1 layout

### Experiment — NORTHWEST corner

This should receive the strongest architectural treatment.

Recommended V3 candidate:
- keep north-wall Matter Bench and west-wall Auxiliary Bench;
- convert the current simple Divider into an L-shaped spatial composition rather than a single screen;
- south boundary: approx. 2.8–3.4m long low storage cabinet (0.75–0.9m high) with slatted or textured-glass upper portion reaching approx. 1.9–2.1m total;
- east boundary: shorter partial divider / shelving monolith approx. 2.2–2.8m long;
- preserve an intentional 1.5–1.8m entrance opening;
- optionally add an overhead open frame/canopy at approx. 2.7–3.0m height, not a closed ceiling;
- use warmer task-lighting later to make the zone feel internally coherent.

The result should read as an atelier / analysis room nested inside the large refuge, not a laboratory cubicle.

### Living / TV — WEST middle

Do not add a wall in front of the sofa.

Use a layered boundary instead:
- increase rug later to roughly 4.8–5.2m x 4.2–4.6m if Human review supports it;
- sofa back becomes the east boundary of the living island;
- add a slim console behind sofa later, approx. 2.4–3.0m long and 0.3–0.4m deep;
- media console / TV wall forms west boundary;
- consider a lightweight overhead 'ceiling raft' / suspended frame aligned with the rug footprint at approx. 2.7–3.0m visual height, leaving the 6m shell visible beyond;
- lighting should form a lower, warmer pool than the general room.

This should make the living area feel like a room within the room without blocking Atlas sightlines.

### Window + Bar + Pantry — NORTH / NORTHEAST

Treat this as one architectural composition, not three loose objects.

Recommended:
- connect Pantry + Bar through common cabinetry language;
- pantry should become a vertical anchor / tall storage block rather than a floating standalone box;
- use the window surround, sill/bar, pantry and possibly a shallow upper/header element to create a framed 'window room';
- keep stool circulation open to the south;
- no full divider between Bar and Retrieval; use negative space and cabinetry edge instead.

The intent is a domestic breakfast/observation niche embedded in the north wall.

### Private Rest — SOUTHEAST

The current single screen is too weak for a 20x20 room.

Recommended V3 candidate:
- create an L-shaped privacy composition around 2 sides of the bed/daybed;
- one side can be slatted / textile / textured glass at approx. 1.8–2.1m;
- the headboard/storage mass should become part of the boundary;
- optionally add a partial overhead canopy/frame at approx. 2.5–2.8m height;
- keep one large diagonal opening toward the room / Outside so it never becomes a conventional bedroom box.

This zone should feel intimate and lower in scale than the 6m shell.

### Dining / Multipurpose Table — SOUTH-CENTRAL

Keep it light.

Recommended:
- no screen;
- use a single pendant / light pool later as the primary vertical marker;
- small rug optional if it does not make the south half too busy;
- the table and four chairs should sit in a defined pocket between Living and Observe rather than float randomly;
- maintain clear gaps around it so the zone is legible through negative space.

### Retrieval — EAST

Do not wall it in.

Gameplay requires deploy direction, tether space, landing space and object handling.

Recommended spatial definition:
- keep a large operational clear zone;
- define the domestic-facing side with a low equipment/storage spine or freestanding architectural console if later needed;
- an overhead portal/frame may be tested only if it does not interfere with guide arm, Orb trajectory or player view;
- lighting / floor glow / material inlay can later carry more of the zoning work than physical partitions.

Retrieval should feel like the place where the house opens toward the Outside, not another enclosed room.

### Atlas

Do not give Atlas a partition.

Atlas should remain the central shared void that several zones orbit. Negative space itself is the boundary language here.

## 5. Important architectural scale principle

Because the main walls are ~6m high, domestic zones should introduce a second visual datum around 2.6–3.0m.

This can be achieved through:
- partial overhead frames;
- suspended lighting rafts;
- upper edges of built-in cabinetry;
- slatted / glass dividers stopping below the main wall height;
- canopy-like elements over Living or Private Rest.

This second datum is important: without it, every chair, sofa and table looks small inside a 6m-high hall.

Do not lower the whole room ceiling. The contrast between the 6m shell and lower human-scale subspaces is desirable.

## 6. Material language for dividers

Keep the palette narrow and residential:

- light or medium warm oak;
- off-white / very light neutral painted surfaces;
- champagne / warm titanium or dark bronze in small amounts;
- ribbed / textured / smoked glass sparingly;
- warm stone / travertine-like material for one or two architectural masses only;
- linen / textile panels possible in Private Rest.

Avoid:
- repeated black metal grids everywhere;
- generic sci-fi glass walls;
- office acoustic partitions;
- industrial safety railings;
- too many unrelated woods/materials.

## 7. Recommended next implementation step

Before final furniture art, perform a dedicated V2.2 / V3 'Zoning Architecture Graybox' pass.

Do not add new gameplay functions.

Only test:
- Experiment L-shaped boundary + optional overhead frame;
- Living sofa-back console + optional ceiling raft/frame;
- Bar/Pantry as one wall composition;
- Private Rest stronger L-shaped boundary + optional canopy;
- Dining light-marker proxy if useful;
- Retrieval low boundary only if it does not affect gameplay.

The review question is not 'are there enough objects?'.

The review question is:

> When standing at human eye height, can the player feel that they are moving from one room-like atmosphere to another without ever losing the openness of the refuge?

Exact partition dimensions, final materials, overhead-frame geometry and lighting remain OPEN pending Human Spatial / Feel Review.
