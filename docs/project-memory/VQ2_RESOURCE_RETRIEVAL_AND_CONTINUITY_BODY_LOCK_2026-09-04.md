## 1. Core resource architecture — LOCKED

The refuge does **not** lose total mass-energy from ordinary internal activity merely because devices are operating.

When the Atlas refuge remains sealed, ordinary internal activity primarily converts stored mass-energy between usable and less-usable states rather than permanently deleting it.

Therefore normal operation such as:

- artificial sun / daylight;
- television;
- workbench use;
- wireless power distribution;
- player movement;
- drone operation;
- plant growth;
- animal metabolism;
- ordinary domestic activity;

must not automatically reduce the long-term refuge total as a permanent tax.

The primary persistent loss occurs when the Atlas boundary is deliberately opened or matter/energy crosses the refuge boundary.

### Core state vocabulary

Use one long-term normalized reserve:

`R = Refuge Reserve`

Initial baseline:

`R = 100`

Important:

- `100` is the starting reference value, not necessarily a hard maximum;
- future Retrieval can raise R above 100;
- Atlas upgrades may increase safe supported capacity;
- the game should avoid presenting R as a simple battery percentage if values above 100 are allowed.

Supporting operating states may include:

- `P = Operational Power / immediately usable power availability`;
- `S = Stability Margin / Atlas ability to stabilize current load`.

P and S are operating states, not additional wallet currencies.

## 2. Retrieval cost rule — LOCKED

Retrieval cost is tied primarily to controlled violation of the Atlas refuge boundary.

Persistent cost should depend conceptually on:

- opening the boundary;
- how long the opening remains active;
- aperture / opening scale;
- matter crossing the boundary;
- unusual instability or anomaly handling.

Orb propulsion, imaging, scanning, local computation, and station operation mainly create temporary power load rather than directly deleting R.

A normal successful Retrieval should often be net-positive in reserve:

`reserve gained from retrieved matter > boundary-opening loss`

However, some discoveries may be deliberately net-negative in raw reserve while remaining worthwhile because they provide:

- knowledge;
- unique components;
- blueprints;
- life;
- relationships;
- Atlas reactions;
- narrative progression;
- future capabilities.

This tradeoff is a core gameplay value decision.

---

## 3. Reserve capacity and overload — LOCKED DIRECTION

R above 100 is allowed and can be desirable.

Do not punish the player immediately for exceeding the starting baseline.

Atlas has a supported capacity that can grow through progression.

If reserve or unusual foreign load exceeds the current safe stabilization capability, the problem is **field load / stability pressure**, not simply “too much money.”

Exact thresholds remain a balancing problem.

### Unintegrated foreign matter

Recently retrieved objects may impose a stabilization burden greater than their simple material value.

Therefore a small anomalous object may be more destabilizing than a large ordinary metal object.

This supports future containment, research, and anomaly-management gameplay.

## 4. Low-reserve behavior — LOCKED DIRECTION

Low R should be expressed primarily through the room physically entering conservation modes rather than through aggressive HUD punishment.

As reserve becomes low, the refuge may progressively reduce or suspend lower-priority systems such as:

- decorative lighting;
- unnecessary drone roaming;
- television / entertainment auto-use;
- high-power fabrication;
- artificial-sun duration/intensity;
- plant growth support;
- nonessential convenience systems;
- normal Retrieval reach / scan frequency.

Priority order should broadly protect:

1. Atlas / refuge stability;
2. human continuity and essential life-support needs;
3. basic local power and mobility;
4. emergency Retrieval capability;
5. optional comfort and entertainment systems.

The design must include a protected reserve floor / emergency recovery path so the player cannot permanently soft-lock a save by spending too much reserve.

Exact thresholds are not yet locked.

## 5. Retrieved-object processing architecture — LOCKED

Retrieved objects must first exist as physical objects in the room.

Do not automatically convert them into inventory currency on arrival.

A recovered object can be examined before the player decides its fate.

The core processing device direction is a combined reclamation/fabrication workstation, working title:

`Matter Reclamation & Fabrication Bench`

Chinese working description:

`物质回收重构台`

Final name is open.

### Core handling actions

Recovered content may be handled through several distinct choices:

- `Preserve` — retain the object intact;
- `Strip` — careful disassembly that favors preserving functional components;
- `Reclaim` — destructive bulk-material reclamation;
- `Research` — preserve/use the object for information or blueprint discovery;
- `Contain` — isolate living, unstable, or anomalous material.

The system must not automatically decide that every retrieved object should be dismantled.

Opportunity cost between keeping, studying, using, containing, and reclaiming an object is a central gameplay principle.

## 6. Generic material families — LOCKED AS CURRENT SYSTEM DIRECTION

Use a deliberately small set of generic bulk feedstocks rather than dozens of ore/mineral currencies.

Current accepted families:

- `Structure` — metals, minerals, ceramics, glass, structural composites;
- `Polymer` — plastics, elastomers, fibers, seals, soft structural materials;
- `Conductive` — conductors, semiconductors, optical/electronic functional material;
- `Bio / Volatile` — water, carbon-bearing organics, gases, nutrient/biological feedstock.

These are material states inside the refuge economy, not abstract money.

Exact names and ratios remain tunable.

### Intact components

Advanced construction must not be reducible to accumulating enough generic feedstock.

Important technology can require:

`generic material + intact recovered component + knowledge/blueprint`

Examples include advanced sensors, Atlas-compatible parts, unusual regulators, biological structures, or specialized recovered mechanisms.

This preserves the value of exploration and discovery.

## 7. Fabrication and construction scale — LOCKED DIRECTION

Construction output should respect object scale.

### Small objects

Can be directly fabricated by the workstation.

Examples:

- tools;
- repair parts;
- lamps;
- sensors;
- small devices.

### Medium objects

Can be fabricated as build packages / modules, then placed using the accepted room placement/grid system.

Examples:

- chair;
- table;
- shelf;
- bed;
- TV cabinet;
- plant module.

### Large objects / infrastructure

Must become visible construction projects rather than instantly appearing from a fabricator.

Examples:

- new wall sections;
- floor/platform expansion;
- containment bay;
- large power infrastructure;
- major Atlas facilities;
- large workshop modules.

Major construction should visibly transform the room and preferably unlock new capabilities.

## 8. Retrieval Orb player-control philosophy — LOCKED

The Orb is an actively stabilized powered device, not a passive bobber.

The player should control **intent**, while the Orb handles low-level stabilization.

Do not require full manual six-degree-of-freedom drone piloting as the default gameplay model.

### Core control flow

- `Aim` — player indicates desired travel direction / target region;
- `Drive / Extend` — Orb actively travels while tether automatically reels out;
- `Hold` — releasing movement causes Orb to stabilize at position;
- `Scan` — deliberate pulse/echo action;
- `Acquire / Contact` — establish interaction with a valid target;
- `Retrieve` — station reels in while Orb actively cooperates with return;
- `Emergency Home` — Orb attempts an automatic safe return to cradle.

The Tether remains a powered umbilical and maximum-reach constraint.

It must not behave like an ordinary gravity-dominated fishing line.

## 9. Echo / radar assistance — LOCKED DIRECTION

Long-distance Retrieval requires spatial assistance beyond direct eyesight.

Use an in-world Retrieval Station display rather than defaulting to a large conventional HUD radar.

Working concept:

`Echo Disc`

Final name is open.

It should communicate at least:

- direction;
- distance;
- relative motion;
- rough mass / size class;
- signal confidence;
- whether biological/anomalous activity may be present;
- unresolved vs increasingly resolved target information;
- tether reach / remaining operating envelope.

Targets should not arrive pre-labeled with loot rarity or full identity.

Information becomes clearer through scan, proximity, and research.

## 10. Retrieval Orb light as state language — LOCKED DIRECTION

The Orb remains a predominantly luminous hero device using warm ivory / yellow-white rather than saturated neon colors.

State communication should rely primarily on:

- brightness;
- pulse frequency;
- pulse shape;
- controlled local motion;
- asymmetric light behavior;
- tether light behavior;

rather than frequent red/green/blue mode switching.

Current state-language direction includes:

- low slow breathing while Stored;
- smooth power-up;
- stable cruise luminance;
- faster breathing near boundary;
- brief bright boundary-crossing event;
- propagating scan pulse;
- target-resolution cue;
- capture double pulse;
- directional tether pulse under tension;
- reduced luminance at low power;
- subtly irregular rhythm around anomalies.

Exact timings and intensities remain Human Visual tuning work.

## 11. Orb camera — LOCKED DIRECTION

Long-range Retrieval may use an Orb camera feed.

Default presentation should keep the player physically and perceptually located in the room.

Preferred presentation:

- physical screen / station display for Orb camera;
- Echo display remains nearby;
- room, station, tether, and player body remain conceptually present;
- fullscreen Orb view may be optional later but should not erase the core fantasy that the player is operating a device from home.

## 12. Player body — LOCKED

Do **not** replace the current user-authored robot-like player with a conventional human model by default.

The player body is redefined as a purpose-built human continuity body.

Working worldbuilding term:

`Human Continuity Shell / Continuity Body`

Final terminology remains open.

The preserving alien civilization transferred/preserved human consciousness into an artificial body designed to support human subjective continuity rather than merely running a human-like AI.

The game should preserve the idea that the player is still a person living a life, despite no longer occupying an ordinary biological human body.

## 13. Human behaviors in the synthetic body — LOCKED DIRECTION

### Eating

Keep eating as a meaningful optional human behavior.

Do not reduce it to a mandatory hunger meter by default.

Eating can support:

- pleasure;
- memory;
- culture;
- social behavior;
- sensory continuity;
- story and discovery.

The Continuity Body may possess taste, smell, oral sensation, temperature perception, and food-processing capability because preserving human life includes preserving lived experience.

### Drinking

Keep drinking.

It may be partly habitual/sensory and partly compatible with body working-fluid or thermal systems, but should not become a constant hydration chore.

### Sleeping

Keep sleep as an important system.

The Continuity Body may require or deliberately perform a low-input integration state analogous to sleep.

Sleep can support:

- memory consolidation fiction;
- day/night progression;
- dreams/memory fragments;
- narrative triggers;
- world-state advancement;
- quiet room changes.

Do not remove sleep simply because the body is artificial.

### Charging

The player should not routinely manage a conventional battery meter while inside the normal room.

The wall-mounted wireless Power Hub provides ordinary continuous power to the Continuity Body within its operating field.

Internal reserve/battery may become meaningful only when the player later leaves normal wireless-power coverage or enters special situations.

## 14. Synthetic-body gameplay — LOCKED DIRECTION

The artificial body can support new gameplay unavailable to a normal human body, especially:

- visible component damage;
- actuator/sensor degradation from real events or risk;
- repair of the player's body through the same material/component economy used for room systems;
- future body capability modules;
- physical consequences that are mechanical rather than generic health-pack abstractions.

However:

- no passive offline body decay;
- no punishment simply for not logging in;
- stronger damage/consequence should come from actual use, risk, exploration, or incidents.

### Continuity rule

Body components may be replaceable, but human consciousness continuity must **not** be treated as a cheap infinitely copyable save file.

The player should feel that this continuing subjective self matters.

The exact metaphysics and technical mechanism remain intentionally unexplained early.

---

## 15. Plants and animals — LOCKED DIRECTION

Plants and animals remain compatible with the closed-system premise.

Their matter does not disappear merely because they metabolize or grow.

They transform internal refuge material between states.

Their primary systemic pressures are throughput, usable power, environment support, and material circulation rather than permanent unexplained deletion of R.

Dead biological material, waste, water, and other outputs can return to the recycling loop.

This preserves the possibility of cat/animal/plant life without introducing arbitrary daily reserve taxes.

---

## 16. Supersession / guardrails

The following should no longer be treated as current core assumptions unless explicitly reintroduced later:

- ordinary device use permanently subtracts from the total 100 reserve;
- the player must maintain a conventional hunger/thirst/battery survival-bar stack;
- every retrieved object automatically becomes money or generic Scrap;
- Coins are the universal solution for the current physical-world Unity gameplay loop;
- dozens of independent ore/resource currencies;
- Retrieval as a bobber/QTE fishing minigame;
- full manual drone flight as the default Orb control scheme;
- swapping the current player to a biological human model solely to preserve eating/drinking/sleep gameplay.

Older XP / Coins / Build Charge research remains historical/proposed material and does not override this new physical-world resource architecture.

## 17. Current integrated core loop

```text
Outside signal / visible opportunity
        ↓
Echo detect / scan / approach
        ↓
Player decides whether opening Atlas is worth the cost
        ↓
Orb crosses Atlas boundary
        ↓
Refuge Reserve suffers real boundary loss
        ↓
Target is acquired and retrieved
        ↓
Physical object enters the room
        ↓
Reserve / stability state changes
        ↓
Inspect
        ↓
Preserve / Strip / Reclaim / Research / Contain
        ↓
Bulk feedstock + intact components + knowledge
        ↓
Fabricate / Build / Repair / Expand
        ↓
Room visibly changes and/or new capability unlocks
        ↓
New classes/depths of Outside become reachable
```

This loop is the current preferred structural spine for VQ2 physical-world gameplay.
