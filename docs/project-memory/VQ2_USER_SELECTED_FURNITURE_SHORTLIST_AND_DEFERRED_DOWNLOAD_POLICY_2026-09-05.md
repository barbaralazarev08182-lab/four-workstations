## Placement-stage procedure

When furniture placement begins, process one asset at a time:

1. Choose the next functional slot in the room (e.g. main sofa, accent chair, coffee table, rug, side table, planter, wall light, nightstand).
2. Return to this shortlist before searching elsewhere.
3. Re-open the exact candidate page.
4. Verify source, author, license, commercial-use conditions, attribution requirements, and whether the model reproduces a real branded/designer product.
5. Download only the chosen asset.
6. Assign a clean project-facing name based on function/style rather than retaining a messy download title.
7. Import into a candidate folder.
8. Validate scale/materials/polycount/texture size in Unity.
9. Place in the real scene and perform Human Visual/Feel Review.
10. Only then move to the next furniture item.

## Important guardrails

- `FREE` in a listing title is not by itself sufficient proof of commercial-game licensing.
- Real branded/designer furniture replicas may be acceptable as prototype/reference assets but can require additional review before final commercial release.
- Do not bulk-import this list now.
- Do not lose or overwrite this shortlist with unrelated assistant recommendations.
